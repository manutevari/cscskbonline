# Competitive CSC/VLE Website Strategy

## Research Baseline

Official CSC ecosystem sources show that CSCs are access points for delivery of Government-to-Citizen services, Digital Seva publishes current launch/news updates, CSC registration focuses on VLE onboarding, and state CSC pages commonly emphasize one-stop access to certificates, forms, and utility payments.

## Competitive Positioning for CSCSKB Online

To compete with generic CSC and VLE websites, CSCSKB Online should combine official-service trust signals with a faster local user experience:

- Official-update headline marquee sourced from CSC-owned websites.
- Clear service cards with eligibility, required documents, fees, processing time, and FAQs.
- Local trust modules: original reviews, Google Maps directions, center owner profile, and visible contact actions.
- AI assistant for service discovery in Hindi and English.
- SEO pages for high-intent local searches such as CSC center near me, online form help, PAN support, certificate services, scheme assistance, and VLE support.
- Admin workflow for urgent announcements, outage notices, new services, and pinned local updates.

## Features That Make It Compete Always

1. Auto-updating official CSC news marquee.
2. Mobile-first sticky call buttons for WhatsApp, call, directions, and AI help.
3. Dedicated pages for every major CSC service.
4. Review/testimonial section with local proof.
5. Schema.org local business metadata.
6. Fast-loading pages with compressed images and minimal plugins.
7. Weekly content refresh using official CSC updates and local service announcements.
8. AI search over services, eligibility, documents, fees, and FAQs.

## WordPress Integration

Install the included WordPress plugin from:

```text
wordpress-plugin/cscskb-csc-news-marquee/cscskb-csc-news-marquee.php
```

Then place this shortcode in the WordPress homepage header, top bar, or Elementor/Block Editor shortcode block:

```text
[cscskb_csc_news_marquee]
```

The plugin checks official CSC sources first and falls back to local CSCSKB announcements if those sites are unavailable from the hosting environment.
